SDR UK Opportunity Fund 
Financial year 2025-2026

The SDR UK Strategic Hub would like to invite proposals from SDR UK data services. We are interested in receiving a range of ideas or opportunities that contribute to the programme’s mission and objectives. Data services may submit individually or as a consortium with one or more other data services. We especially welcome proposals for activities that bring together two or more data services to collaborate on areas that have been identified as programme priorities, including but not limited to those discussed at the May 2025 SDR UK Leadership Group meeting. 

SDR UK opportunity funding can be used to support extension/enhancement of existing activities AND/OR new activities, including:
•	Infrastructure development, e.g. platform integration, data acquisition, new data products, etc. 
•	Research projects the support infrastructure development, e.g. developing new tools and methods for analysing smart data, piloting the utility of new datasets or data products, testing new infrastructure, etc. 

Data services may lead or be involved in more than one proposal. Collaborative proposals should submit a single proposal document, but include two or more costing templates for each data service involved. Justification for resources can provide an overview of the breakdown of costs and resources between data services. ESRC expects the appropriate collaboration agreements to be in place between data services on joint projects.

The SDR UK Strategic Hub will assess proposals and make funding decisions, with support provided from the SDR UK Programme Oversight Board.

Funding for successful proposals will be delivered in Q3 and/or Q4 of financial year 2025/26. We recognise that, in order for activities to align with existing data service priorities, it may be necessary for projects to be delivered over a longer timescale. 

Successful proposals will be required to report delivery as part of the existing data services quarterly highlight reporting process.
